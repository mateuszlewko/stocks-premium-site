Stocks Premium Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Stocks Premium is provided by Mateusz Lewko. You can use the app without creating an account or connecting a brokerage.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your watchlist, recent symbols, display preferences, Live Activity settings, earnings reminder choices, and calculator inputs on your device. Stocks Premium and its widgets share the information needed for widget display through device-local storage. The app also caches some market data and company logos. Earnings reminders are scheduled through your device’s notification system. Your operating system settings determine whether these reminders are shown.

Connections to market data and news services

The app and its widgets make requests directly to data providers to retrieve quotes, charts, financial statements, fund holdings, earnings dates, news, and logos. These requests can include search text, requested ticker symbols or dates, and network information such as your IP address. Yahoo Finance, Stock Analysis, Nasdaq, Seeking Alpha, news publishers, and their content delivery services may receive such requests and handle them under their own privacy policies. Market-data requests can take place when a widget refreshes or when the system allows background quote refresh.

When you open a news article or a social link, the destination site may receive your visit and process data under its own privacy policy. Market news can include links to publisher websites, Reddit, and X.

Your choices

Watchlist and preference data stays on your device so the app can restore it across launches. You can remove watchlist symbols, clear recent searches, and cancel earnings reminders in the app. Cached logos have a 14-day expiration and can be evicted earlier to make room. Use system Settings to manage app storage and turn off notifications or Live Activities. You can remove widgets from the Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact stockspremium@ikapital.app.

Website and support

This website uses no analytics scripts, forms, or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process network and request information to operate it. If you contact support, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond and resolve the issue. Please leave out brokerage credentials and personal financial details.
